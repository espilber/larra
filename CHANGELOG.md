# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Larra (fork of Rebost 0.9.3)

First Larra release. This is a fork of
[Rebost](https://github.com/Frontierz-AI/Rebost) 0.9.3, renamed and continued
under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Everything Rebost 0.9.3 shipped is here: Chat, Shelves,
Recipes, House rules, Privacy Lens, local engine, and macOS/Windows targets.

Planned on this fork:

- Connect to a local OpenAI-compatible endpoint (LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp
  server) instead of installing a local AI.
- An embedded office-editing environment built from ONLYOFFICE, downloaded on
  first use.
- Multimodal chat over documents: Office files, images (OCR and vision-capable
  models), audio and video (model-permitting).
