# Branding

The name **Larra** and the marks in `docs/assets/` identify this open-source project.

Official description: **Private AI that works with your files. What happens on your computer stays on your computer.**

Either sentence can stand on its own: the first where space is tight, the second where the point is privacy.

The name honours Mariano José de Larra (*Fígaro*), the Spanish chronicler who
wrote about the world from his desk. Larra is the local writer that lives on
your computer: it reads your documents, answers your questions, and helps you
edit what is yours — without any of it leaving the machine.

User-facing English names the outcome rather than the machinery, and prefers AI over model. Product words are Shelf, Recipe, Chat, and House rules.

## Origin

Larra is a fork of [Rebost](https://github.com/Frontierz-AI/Rebost) ("pantry"
in Catalan), maintained at [espilber/larra](https://github.com/espilber/larra)
under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Inherited code remains MIT; see [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).
Factual references are fine: "a fork of Rebost", "based on Rebost".

## Trademark

The AGPL license in [LICENSE](../LICENSE) covers the code. It does not grant rights to the Larra name or to the marks in `docs/assets/`.

The name should not imply that something is this project when it is not. Give a fork or a separate service its own name and icon, so nobody has to guess whether it is maintained here.
